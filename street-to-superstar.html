How to shoot

Press and hold anywhere on the court. The white marker glides up the power bar and back down for as long as you hold.

Let go in the green for a perfect shot. Yellow often rattles in. Red misses.

The green moves higher for longer shots. Consistency slows the marker; every upgrade widens the green.

Three in a row puts you on fire: every basket is worth double until you miss.

Threes score 3, everything else 2. A swish adds 1.
