Privacy

HhGames has no accounts, no adverts and no tracking. The only things saved online are what you choose to post: a review, or your name next to a score.

What's saved online

Reviews
The game, your star rating, the name you type and your review. Harry checks each review before it's shown to everyone.
Leaderboard scores
The game, the name you type and your score, only when you tap Add to leaderboard. After that, your new best scores are added automatically.
Play counts
A total for each game that goes up by one when someone starts playing. Nothing about who played is saved.

These are stored with Supabase, the database service HhGames uses. Use a first name or a nickname, never your full name, address, school or anything else personal.

What stays on your device

  • Game progress, coins, unlocks and settings are saved in your browser on your own phone or computer. They're never sent anywhere.
  • HhGames remembers the name you last used and your best scores, so it only asks about real new bests.
  • Clearing your browser's site data removes all of this.

Other services

The website and games are hosted on GitHub Pages, and the fonts come from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services see basic connection details such as your IP address when the page loads.

Removing something

If you'd like a review or score taken down, ask Harry Davies, who runs HhGames, and he'll delete it.
